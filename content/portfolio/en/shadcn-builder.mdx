## Why I built it

Building forms is one of the most repetitive parts of front-end work: the same inputs, labels, layouts and validation, over and over again. I wanted a tool that lets me assemble a form visually and then hands me code I would have written myself.

## What it does

- **20+ form components** and **100+ pre-built templates**, organised by category.
- **Responsive layouts** that I can design visually on a canvas.
- **Code export** to clean React + Tailwind CSS that uses shadcn/ui components, ready to paste into a project.

## Open source and community

Shadcn Builder is **open source on GitHub**, where it has grown to more than 600 stars. I launched it on Product Hunt, and the community regularly sends me feedback and feature requests, such as form previews and validation settings, which shape the roadmap.
