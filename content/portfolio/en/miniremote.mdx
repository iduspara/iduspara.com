## Why I built it

I run a headless Mac mini, and I wanted to reach it from my laptop, my iPhone or my iPad as if I were sitting in front of it. Existing tools felt either heavy, account-driven or blurry on Retina screens. So I built the remote desktop I wanted to use myself.

## What it does

- **Smooth, sharp streaming** at up to 60 fps, rendered Retina-sharp at the size of my window.
- **No account required.** I connect with a Host ID and a password, and that's it.
- **A virtual screen for headless Macs**, so a Mac mini without a monitor still has a proper desktop.
- **Shared clipboard** for text, images and files up to 60 MB, plus sound streaming, screenshots and screen recording.
- **Touch and trackpad control** on iPhone and iPad, including Split View and Stage Manager.
- **Automatic discovery and reconnection** on the local network, and a menu bar app that can keep the Mac awake.

## Built for AI agents

MiniRemote ships with an **MCP server**. Agents such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor or LM Studio can see the screen, click, type and open apps, on a separate virtual screen, so they never get in my way while I keep working.

## Security

Connections are encrypted and go directly between devices whenever possible, with a relay as fallback. Repeated wrong passwords lock the host out.

## Business model

I offer MiniRemote **free on the home network** with unlimited viewers. A Pro subscription or a one-time Lifetime licence unlocks remote access from anywhere and AI agent use on up to three Macs.
