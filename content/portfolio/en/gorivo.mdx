## Why I built it

Fuel prices in Croatia are published officially, but they're hard to compare on the go. I wanted to open one page and immediately see where the cheapest fuel near me is.

## What it does

- **Every station nearby**, sorted by price and distance, with the official price per litre.
- **An interactive map** of all stations, built on OpenStreetMap.
- **Filters** for fuel type (Eurodiesel, Eurosuper 95/100, LPG and more), distance, provider and motorway stations.
- **Price history** and pages for each city, so drivers can see how prices change over time.
- **A blog** with news about fuel prices.

## Data

All prices come from the official source, the Croatian **Ministry of Economy** (Ministarstvo gospodarstva). I keep the data up to date and show when each price took effect.
